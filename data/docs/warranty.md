# Warranty Policy

_Applies to all purchases regardless of purchase year._

## Standard warranty

All Nordwind Outdoor products carry a **2-year warranty** against
manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date.

## Lifetime warranty

**Backpacks and duffel bags** carry a **lifetime warranty** on seams, zippers
and buckles. Normal wear of fabric and padding is not covered.

## Electronics

Electronics (GPS units, headlamps, watches) carry the standard 2-year
warranty. Battery capacity degradation is covered only in the first
**6 months**.

## What is not covered

- Damage from misuse, falls, or unauthorized repairs.
- Normal wear and tear (abrasion, UV fading, sole wear on footwear).
- Products bought from unauthorized resellers.

## How to claim

Warranty claims are separate from returns: a warranty claim can be filed at
any time within the warranty period, even years after the return window has
closed. File a claim through the support portal with photos and the order
number. Approved claims result in repair, replacement, or store credit at our
discretion.
