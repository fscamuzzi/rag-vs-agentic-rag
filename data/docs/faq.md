# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Only before it is processed (before 14:00 CET same day). After that, refuse
the delivery or start a return.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes, €3.50 per item, selectable at checkout.

## Returns vs warranty

**My product broke after the return window — am I out of luck?**
No. Returns and warranty claims are different things. The return window
(45/60 days in 2025) is for changing your mind; the warranty (2 years, or
lifetime for backpack seams/zippers/buckles) covers manufacturing defects and
can be claimed at any time within the warranty period.

**I lost my receipt — can I still return?**
Yes, if you ordered through your account we have the order on file.

## Payments

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank
transfer for orders over €500.

**When am I charged?**
At shipment, not at order placement.
