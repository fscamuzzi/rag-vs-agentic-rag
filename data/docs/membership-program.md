# Nordwind+ Membership Program

_Launched February 2025._

## Tiers

| Tier | Annual fee | Requirements |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Silver | Free | Any registered account |
| Gold | €49/year | — |
| Platinum | €99/year | Invitation after €2,000 lifetime spend |

## Benefits

### Silver
- Early access to seasonal sales.
- Birthday discount code (10%).

### Gold
- Everything in Silver.
- **Refunds always to the original payment method** (no store credit default).
- Free expedited shipping on orders over €50.
- Extended return window: **60 days** (as per the 2025 return policy for all
  Nordwind+ members).

### Platinum
- Everything in Gold.
- Dedicated support line with 4-hour response SLA.
- Free gear tune-up once a year (ski edges, tent waterproofing).

## Notes

Membership benefits apply only to purchases made **while the membership is
active**. Benefits are not retroactive to earlier purchases.
