# Return Policy — 2025 (current)

_Effective January 1, 2025. Replaces the 2024 return policy._

## Return window

- Standard customers: **45 days** from delivery (was 30 days in 2024).
- **Nordwind+ members: 60 days** from delivery, regardless of tier.
- Electronics (GPS units, headlamps, watches): **14 days**, but as of 2025
  **opened electronics are accepted** if all accessories are included.

## Condition requirements

- Original packaging is recommended but **no longer required**.
- The **restocking fee has been removed** for all product categories,
  including opened items.
- Items showing wear from field use are evaluated case by case.

## Refund method

The default refund method is now **store credit**, issued within 3 business
days. Customers can request a refund to the original payment method instead;
those refunds take up to 10 business days.

Nordwind+ Gold and Platinum members always get refunds to the original
payment method by default.

## Exclusions

- Gift cards remain non-refundable.
- Personalized products remain non-refundable.
- Safety equipment (climbing ropes, harnesses, carabiners) cannot be returned
  once the security seal is broken.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is **free for all customers** in 2025 via prepaid label.
