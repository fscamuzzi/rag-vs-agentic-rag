# Return Policy — 2024 (superseded)

_Effective January 1, 2024 — December 31, 2024. Superseded by the 2025 policy._

## Return window

Customers may return any product within **30 days** of delivery. The return
window starts on the day the carrier marks the parcel as delivered.

## Condition requirements

- Products must be returned in their original packaging.
- **Opened items are accepted**, but a **10% restocking fee** is deducted from
  the refund.
- Used or damaged items are not eligible for return (see the Warranty policy
  for defects).

## Refund method

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within 10 business days
of the returned item passing inspection at our warehouse.

## Exclusions

- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Personalized products (custom engraving, custom sizing) are non-refundable.
- Electronics must be returned **within 14 days** and unopened; opened
  electronics cannot be returned under this policy.

## Return shipping

Return shipping costs are paid by the customer, except when the return is due
to our error (wrong item shipped, item damaged in transit).
