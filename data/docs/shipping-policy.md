# Shipping Policy

## Delivery options

| Option | Cost | Delivery time |
|--------|------|---------------|
| Standard | €4.90 (free over €79) | 3–5 business days |
| Expedited | €9.90 | 1–2 business days |
| Pickup point | €2.90 | 3–5 business days |

## Coverage

We ship to all EU countries, Norway, Switzerland and the UK. Customs duties
for non-EU destinations are the customer's responsibility.

## Order processing

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on business days are processed the same day.
Orders are not processed on weekends or Italian public holidays.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is considered lost if tracking shows no movement for 7 consecutive
days. In that case we ship a replacement immediately at no cost — the
customer does not need to wait for the carrier investigation to finish.
